MY APPROACH

A website should be more than a nice first impression. It should be clear, useful and memorable.

I enjoy the whole journey — understanding what a project needs, shaping how it looks, writing the code and polishing the small interactions. I'm still growing, and that's part of the fun: every project gives me something new to learn.

HOW I WORK

Three things I
come back to.

Not a rigid formula — just the principles I use to make better decisions while building.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the people

Understand what the site is for, who will use it and what they should be able to do next.

02 / MAKE

Design with intent

Use hierarchy, colour, rhythm and movement to support the message instead of distracting from it.

03 / REFINE

Care about the finish

Test small screens, tidy up details and make the interactions feel consistent from start to end.
